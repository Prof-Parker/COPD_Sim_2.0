If a phone is frozen or out of step

Puts every connected phone in this team back on the team's current screen. Safe to press at any time.

Use when a seat shows online but the student's phone is stuck or gone. It frees the seat so they can scan a QR above. Refreshing the old phone also brings it back.

What to try, by problem
  • One student dropped. They usually reconnect on their own within a few seconds of unlocking the phone. If not: refresh the page, or scan the partner's QR Invite button or the QR above. Meanwhile the partner can tap Continue anyway.
  • Both students dropped. Show Mr. Holland's QR to the patient and the Observer's QR to the observer, so each gets their own role back.
  • Phone died or was swapped. Scan that role's QR on the replacement phone. It takes over the seat with the score and screen intact.
  • "Rejoining your team…" won't finish. The old phone still counts as connected. Press Disconnect for that role, and the waiting phone gets in within a few seconds.
  • The two phones show different screens, or a button won't respond. Press Resync both phones.
  • A student is on the wrong team (scanned the class QR again). Have them scan their own team's QR here, then remove the stray team below.
